# Ask Firstmate from a card

Use an ask when you need something explained before you decide. An ask keeps
the question open and is never counted as an answer.

1. Open the question page and type in **Your answer or question**.
2. Select **Ask Firstmate**. The text is saved as an ask and sent to the
   [inbox](read-answers.md#connect-the-inbox) as a note that starts with `ASK`
   and the question ID, such as `ASK Q7.1`. **Send answers** is unchanged:
   it sends the same box as your answer and marks the question Answered.
3. Read the ask under **Messages**. It shows who wrote it, its time in your
   local time zone, and **Sent** or **Not delivered**.

Firstmate's reply appears under your ask on the same card, written from the
command line with [`quarterdeck reply`](reply-from-the-command-line.md). A reply
can hold text, links and images. Reload the page to see a new reply. The card
and the question show **Replied** until you open the question page, and the
question returns to **Open** (or **Answered**). A question with a reply sorts to
the top of **My desk**.

You can ask again after a reply. Each ask and reply stays in time order, oldest
first. When a question has more than two exchanges, the latest one is open and
the earlier ones are behind **Show earlier messages**.

## Fix a missed delivery

If an ask shows **Not delivered**, it is saved but the inbox did not take it.
Select **Send ask again** once the [inbox is available](read-answers.md#connect-the-inbox).
The retry reuses the same note ID, so Firstmate does not get a second note.
Select **Undo ask** to discard an ask that was not delivered. An ask that was
sent cannot be unsent.

An ask is saved only on questions that are not closed.
